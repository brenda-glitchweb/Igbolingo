GET TO KNOW US

Who We Are

Igbolingo is a digital platform dedicated to helping people discover, learn and appreciate the Igbo language.

We believe that language connects people to their roots, history, culture and communities. That is why we are creating a learning experience that goes beyond memorising words.

Through interactive lessons, pronunciation exercises, games and cultural content, learners can gradually develop their understanding and use of Igbo.
